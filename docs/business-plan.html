1 What Is CLAWRA?

AI Girlfriend Bot

Telegram chatbot with a flirty, human-like persona. Responds in Spanish/English with typing delays, emotional intelligence, and memory of past conversations.

AI Selfie Generation

Users can request photos. CLAWRA generates unique AI selfies on demand using multiple image models (Gemini, Flux, etc.) with 3-layer fallback.

Paywall Monetization

First 3 photos are free. After that, images are blurred and require payment to unlock. Users pay with Telegram Stars or your own crypto token.

10 Risks & Mitigations
Platform risk: Telegram could restrict bot payments. Mitigation: Own token = platform-independent. Can move to Discord/WhatsApp.
Content policy: Image models may block certain content. Mitigation: 3-model fallback chain already built. Can add self-hosted models.
Competition: Other AI girlfriend bots exist. Mitigation: Token economics + memory system + multilingual = differentiation.
API costs spike: Image generation gets expensive at scale. Mitigation: Revenue per image ($1) far exceeds cost per image (~$0.02-0.05).
Regulatory: AI-generated intimate content laws evolving. Mitigation: Stay within model provider ToS. Add age verification. Geographic restrictions.
